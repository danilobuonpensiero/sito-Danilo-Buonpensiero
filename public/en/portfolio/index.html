Projects and simulations

The testing ground.

Projects developed during the IULM Master in Sports Marketing and Communication and the Alma Mater advanced course. Not client work: the space where I applied the same methods to real or simulated briefs.

VSME/EFRAG sustainability report
Bologna Sport Marathon SSD · Alma Mater Studiorum
Jun 2026 → Jul 2026
What I did
  • The organisation’s first sustainability report, drafted according to the VSME standard published by EFRAG for small and medium enterprises.
  • The full reporting cycle: mapping the organisation’s activities and systematically collecting environmental, social and governance data.
  • Selecting the indicators relevant to the scope and liaising with internal functions to validate the information.
  • Drafting the document and shaping the narrative of its contents.
  • Adapting indicators to the context of mass sporting events, where reporting is still rare: participant mobility, waste management along the route, territorial impact and social effects on the local community.
