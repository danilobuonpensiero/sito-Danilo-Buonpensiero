ABOUT
Understand a story. Bring it to the right people.

My path started in publishing, back when communicating meant writing: finding a story, verifying it, delivering it to an audience. From there the job broadened by degrees — first towards press office work and media relations, then towards digital content and social, and finally towards the strategy that ties the two together.

Nine years on, the substance hasn’t changed: understand a story and bring it to the right people. What’s changed are the tools and the scale — from press rooms to social feeds, from a single article to a brand’s full editorial plan.

I ran communication and press office for Kings League, an international sports entertainment league. I contributed to the content strategy of OTT platform Mediaset Infinity. I helped shape the editorial identity of Pianeta Serie B, a publishing platform with a community of hundreds of thousands of followers.
